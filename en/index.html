  1. First contactWe talk through the situation and the person's request.
  2. Energy readingI look at the state of the aura and the particulars of the situation.

The philosophy of SPIRIT

I am convinced that behind an outward event there is often a deeper story. So it matters to see not only what is happening now, but also the connections that brought a person to this point.

My approach rests on individual reading, clairvoyance, rune analysis and a choice of practices that fits the specific situation.

I do not reduce the work to one reading or one universal solution. It matters to me to look at a person and their circumstances as a whole, to determine what needs attention, and to choose the suitable direction of the session.

SPIRIT is my gift, my spiritual path and my individual practice.
