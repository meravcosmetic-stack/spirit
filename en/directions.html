SPIRIT: directions of individual work

Every situation is individual. The work begins by reading a person's energetic state through the aura and looking at the circumstances of their life. Depending on the request and on what is seen, the suitable session format is chosen.

Clairvoyance and rune readings

When it matters to understand what is happening, to see the possible causes of a situation, or to look at relationships, business, finances or a choice ahead.

I give individual rune readings, analyse connections and repeating circumstances, and look at the possible directions in which a situation may develop.

Energy cleansing and releasing blocks

For those who feel a loss of strength, constant obstacles, stagnation and unexplained changes in life.

I read the state of the aura and look at possible energy blocks and negative influences. In esoteric practice these topics also include curses and hexes (porcha and podklad). Conclusions are not drawn in advance, only after an individual look at the situation.

Depending on the request, an energy cleansing is done, with the relevant work with runes and Reiki.

Opening roads

When the usual movement of life seems to have stopped: business is not developing, opportunities do not appear, income shrinks, plans do not come true.

During the session I look at the causes of stagnation and do rune and energy work aimed at working through obstacles and opening new paths in life.

Lineage and ancestral programs

Repeating family scenarios, ancestral themes and their possible influence on relationships, finances, personal life and life path.

In this practice we look at ancestral programs, work with themes that need completion, and turn to the strength and blessing of the lineage.

Relationships and personal situations

If a relationship has reached a dead end, the same stories keep repeating, or there are situations you cannot bring to a close.

With clairvoyance and rune readings we look at circumstances, connections and repeating scenarios. Depending on the request, the work may include energy practices and the closing of gestalts.

Finances and business

When a business loses its former pace, income shrinks and money seems to slip through the fingers.

I look at the situation through clairvoyance and rune readings, paying attention to possible energy and ancestral blocks, repeating scenarios and obstacles. When needed, I add cleansing and the practices of opening roads.

Reiki and work with chakras

A separate direction of my practice: individual Reiki sessions.

During a session I pay attention to the chakras, the energy centres, possible blocks and the general state of a person's energy. The practice is aimed at energy work, cleansing and restoring balance.

I hold initiation in two directions of Reiki: Reiki of the chakras (work with the chakras: filling and opening them) and Kundalini Reiki.

Mediumship and communication with souls

Mediumship is a special part of my ancestral gift and an independent direction of SPIRIT.

I hold mediumship sessions involving communication with souls, for people who consciously choose to turn to this particular practice.

The format and content of such a session are agreed with the person beforehand.
